About Our Cooperative

Abavubyi Cooperative started jokingly among friends but later grew into a successful enterprise. What began as simple fun turned into a strong community of people working together.

Our cooperative focuses on unity, collaboration, and sharing good moments while building something meaningful together.

We are proudly located in Kigali Province, Rwanda.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide our stomachs with the best high quality food while strengthening our friendship and building a strong cooperative that benefits all its members.
