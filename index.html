About Us

Abavubyi Cooperative started jokingly between friends but has grown into a successful enterprise. Our goal is to create a strong community while enjoying high quality food together.

We are proudly located in Kigali Province, Rwanda and we welcome new members who share our values of unity, teamwork and enjoyment of life.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to provide our stomachs with the best high quality food while building a strong cooperative that supports friendship and collaboration.
